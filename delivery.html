Shipping & Delivery Policy

100% Digital Delivery

iRA Digi HUB is a completely digital platform. We do not sell physical products, and therefore, no physical shipping or courier services are involved.

How You Will Receive Your Product

  • Instant Screen Access: Immediately after a successful payment, you will be redirected to a success page containing your secure download link (Google Drive or direct file).
  • Email Delivery: A backup link along with your payment invoice will be instantly sent to the email address you provide during checkout.

Delivery Time

Digital delivery is instant (within 1-5 minutes of payment confirmation). If you do not receive your email within this timeframe, please check your Spam/Junk folder.

Since there is no physical shipping, you will not be charged any shipping fees, handling fees, or delivery charges during checkout.
